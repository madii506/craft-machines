Which models do the machines use?

Krea 2 (great for photos, scenes and characters) and Ideogram 3.0 (great for text, logos, memes and posters). Each craft picks one when it’s made. Every picture is made fresh by that model at the moment you press run.

How many runs does a machine get?

Every live craft gets 20 free runs a day, 60 while its token is trading and 150 once it graduates. The house also keeps one daily cap across all machines. Counts reset at 00:00 UTC, and a run that fails isn’t counted.

Who holds the money?

Nobody here. pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee. Before you sign, the page reads the transactions and stops if the split isn’t the one shown. Anyone can push waiting fees out to everyone with one click.

How is the guild drawn?

The moment a craft is recorded, up to 8 $CRAFT guild members are drawn, seeded by Solana’s latest finalized blockhash. Tickets: apprentice 1x, journeyman 1.5x (day 3), artisan 2x (day 10), master 3x (day 30). Until anyone has joined, the guild’s 15% stays with the maker.

What can’t the machines make?

Nothing sexual, nothing with children, no gore or hate. The models screen every request too. Real people’s faces and other brands’ logos are a bad idea: don’t make a machine for them.

Where do the coins in Live come from?

Straight from pump.fun: new coins stream in the moment they’re created, and Trending reads Jupiter’s list of the most traded pump.fun tokens in the last hour. Their pictures are their own.
